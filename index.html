    How the model works

    Five modules, one chain

    Each module is a set of plain calculations with every coefficient visible. A change to any input flows through to the finances and the impact ledger. Ranges shown under each headline figure reflect the uncertainty in the underlying coefficients.

    1

    Site & demand

    Population, anchor users and services set the compute the site must deliver.

    demand_kW = base + users × W_per_user
    2

    Technical design

    Cooling choice and climate set PUE and WUE; IT load sets racks and equipment.

    facility_kW = IT_kW × PUE
    3

    Energy & water

    Solar and battery sizing, grid draw, emissions, and water on site and at the power station.

    solar_kWp = kWh/day × share ÷ (PSH × derate) water = IT_MWh×WUE + grid_MWh×EWIF
    4

    Financial model

    Capex, opex, revenue and debt. In Assess mode: rates, reliefs, grid costs and who pays them.

    net = revenue − opex − debt service
    5

    Impact ledger

    Eight domains scored for benefit and burden, with a checklist of questions to settle.

    score = f(outputs, local context)
